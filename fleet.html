Fleet Risk Analytics

Evaluated whether an existing fleet risk score sufficiently identified unsafe driving behavior.

Hadoop / HDFS · Pig · Hive / Impala · Power BI · Python

Business question

Evaluated whether an existing fleet risk score sufficiently identified unsafe driving behavior.

Approach & technical work

The team documented a workflow from CSV ingestion through Hadoop and Pig transformations to Hive/Impala querying, Power BI reporting, and Python analysis.

Findings & value

The presentation reported 7 of 25 drivers above the high-risk threshold and identified limitations in distinguishing medium- and high-risk drivers.
