Price Sensitivity & Consumer Behavior

Investigated which factors were associated with order cancellations among returning customers after a free-delivery trial.

SAS · ETL · Logistic regression · Exploratory analysis

Business question

Investigated which factors were associated with order cancellations among returning customers after a free-delivery trial.

Approach & technical work

Prepared loyalty and non-loyalty datasets, standardized identifiers and timestamps, removed duplicates and authorization-only charges, and analyzed cancellation drivers using logistic regression.

Findings & value

The report identified an approximately 16.8% cancellation rate among returning non-loyalty customers in the analyzed sample.

Publication note: Collaborative academic project; conclusions describe the sample, not a production experiment. This is a concise portfolio summary; supporting originals have not been publicly uploaded.
